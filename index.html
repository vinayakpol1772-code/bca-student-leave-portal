Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common queries regarding BCA Department leave guidelines.

Is uploading a Medical Certificate mandatory for Medical Leave?
Yes. The system enforces medical certificate verification. If you select "Medical Leave", you must upload a doctor's certificate in PDF, JPG, JPEG, or PNG format (Max 5MB). Applications without an attached document cannot be submitted.
How long does it take for leave approval?
Normally, requests are reviewed by the Class Counselor within 12-24 hours and forwarded to the BCA HOD for final approval.
Where can I download my Leave Approval Letter?
Once your application status changes to "Approved", a "Download Letter" button will appear in your Student Dashboard under Leave History.
Can I withdraw or cancel a pending leave request?
Yes. As long as your application is in "Pending Faculty" or "Pending HOD" state, you can click the withdrawal icon in your history table to cancel it.
